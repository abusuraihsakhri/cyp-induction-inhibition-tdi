Equation and interpretation

k_obs = k_inact × [I] / (K_I + [I])
Activity (%) = 100 × exp(−k_obs × time)

This calculation assumes one-site saturable time-dependent inactivation, constant inhibitor concentration, and first-order activity loss. It does not fit parameters from experiments, account for enzyme turnover or inhibitor depletion, classify reversible inhibition or induction, or predict clinical drug–drug interactions. The repository's separate Python audit/CLI workflows use heuristic screening thresholds, not these kinetic equations.

Input values and results remain in your browser. The CSV export is created locally; no data are uploaded. For research and educational exploration only, not clinical decision-making.
